# Typed Decision Reliability Research Study

Research materials and a completed CPU-only feasibility pilot for **Do Frozen Confidence Gates Survive Temporal Shift?**

The pilot uses official CFPB complaint narratives to predict the original Issue label within Debt collection, with chronological splits and TF-IDF + logistic regression. It includes duplicate/template audits, keyword ablation, probability metrics, and selective prediction evaluation.

The prespecified feasibility decision was **GO (4/4 conditions passed)**. This is permission to consider a larger experiment, not evidence that Laya works or that a full paper is novel. No Laya or transformer training was performed. Later cohorts have already been examined during this pilot and must not be described as untouched confirmatory data.

Downloaded third-party sources retain their original rights and terms. CFPB labels are administrative/consumer-selected labels, not expert-adjudicated semantic ground truth; published complaints are not representative of all consumers.

The next neural stage now has a [frozen protocol](research-project/research/full_protocol.md), [analysis plan](research-project/research/preregistration_next_stage.md), [GPU runbook](research-project/research/neural_experiment_plan.md), and [Credit Card replication audit](research-project/research/credit_card_replication_audit.md). Preparation does not execute neural training. The earlier pilot reports remain historical artifacts.
